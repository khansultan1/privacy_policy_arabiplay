# Privacy Policy for ArabiPlay

**Effective Date:** August 12, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 12, 2026

ArabiPlay ("we", "us", or "our") is a children’s educational application designed to help children learn Arabic through interactive games, activities, stories, and educational content.

We respect the privacy of children and their parents and are committed to protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how ArabiPlay handles information when you use our application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information

ArabiPlay does not require children to create an account, provide their real name, email address, phone number, home address, or other direct personal information to use the core educational features of the application.

We do not intentionally collect personal information from children unless it is necessary for a specific feature and handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

Depending on the features and services enabled in the application, limited technical information may be collected automatically, such as:

* Device type and operating system
* Application version
* Language and general device settings
* Anonymous or pseudonymous application usage information
* Crash reports and diagnostic information
* General information about how features are used

This information is used to:

* Improve application performance
* Fix bugs and crashes
* Understand which educational activities are useful
* Improve the user experience
* Maintain application security and reliability

We do not use this information to identify a child personally.

## 2. Children’s Privacy

ArabiPlay is designed for children and families.

We do not knowingly collect or request unnecessary personal information from children.

We do not require children to provide:

* Their full name
* Home address
* Telephone number
* Email address
* Social media account
* Precise location
* Financial information

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of the application and review the privacy settings available on their device.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate parental involvement, please contact us at the email address below. We will take reasonable steps to investigate and delete the information where appropriate.

## 3. Advertising

If advertising is enabled in ArabiPlay, we will use advertising providers that support child-directed applications and applicable privacy requirements.

Advertising may be limited to contextual or otherwise permitted advertising appropriate for children.

We do not knowingly allow personalized advertising based on a child's personal information.

If advertising is not enabled in your version of ArabiPlay, no advertising information is collected for advertising purposes.

## 4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We may use third-party services to help us understand application performance and identify crashes or technical problems.

These services may process limited technical information such as:

* Device information
* Application version
* Crash information
* Anonymous usage statistics
* General diagnostic information

Where such services are used with ArabiPlay, we configure them in a manner appropriate for a children’s application and applicable privacy requirements.

## 5. Third-Party Services

ArabiPlay may use third-party services for functions such as analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud services, app distribution, or other technical functionality.

Third-party providers may process limited information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Examples of services that may be used include:

* Google Play Services
* Firebase services
* Google AdMob, if advertising is enabled
* Other infrastructure or analytics providers required to operate the application

The specific services used by a particular version of ArabiPlay may change as the application develops.

## 6. Location Information

ArabiPlay does not require precise GPS location information for its core educational features.

We do not intentionally collect a child's precise location.

If a future feature requires location information, we will update this Privacy Policy and implement appropriate privacy protections before collecting such information.

## 7. Microphone and Voice Features

If ArabiPlay provides voice-based educational activities, the application may request microphone permission.

Microphone access is used only when a user actively starts a voice-related feature.

Where voice recordings are used:

* The microphone is not continuously monitored.
* Recording is activated only through the relevant feature.
* We do not use children's voices for advertising.
* We do not sell voice recordings.
* We will clearly explain when a feature requires microphone access.

If voice data is processed by a third-party service, the applicable third-party privacy practices will also apply.

## 8. Artificial Intelligence Features

Some future or current ArabiPlay features may use artificial intelligence to provide educational functionality.

If AI functionality processes user-provided content, we will take reasonable measures to minimize the amount of information transmitted and avoid unnecessary collection of personal information from children.

We do not intentionally use children's personal information to train commercial AI models without appropriate authorization and legal basis.

## 9. How We Use Information

Information that we collect or receive may be used to:

* Provide and maintain ArabiPlay
* Deliver educational features
* Improve learning activities
* Diagnose technical problems
* Prevent abuse or fraudulent activity
* Improve application security
* Understand general application usage
* Comply with applicable legal requirements

We do not sell children's personal information.

## 10. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Technical and diagnostic information may be retained for a limited period to help us maintain and improve the application.

If personal information is received and we determine that it was collected inappropriately from a child, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

## 11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 12. Parents and Guardians

Parents and legal guardians may contact us to:

* Ask what personal information may have been collected
* Request deletion of personal information
* Ask questions about our privacy practices
* Report concerns about a child's privacy
* Request additional information about third-party services used by the application

Please contact us using the information provided below.

## 13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding personal information, including:

* The right to know what information is collected
* The right to request access to information
* The right to request deletion
* The right to request correction
* The right to object to certain processing
* The right to withdraw consent where applicable

Parents and guardians may exercise applicable rights on behalf of their children.

## 14. International Users

ArabiPlay may be used by families in different countries.

If information is processed by third-party service providers located in another country, that information may be transferred and processed in accordance with applicable laws and the provider's privacy practices.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that children's privacy is protected when information is processed internationally.
